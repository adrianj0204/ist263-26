Why Do People Watch Horror Movies?

Horror movies provide a thrill-seeking emotional release, allowing viewers to experience suspense and fear within a controlled, safe environment.

A Short History of Horror

  • Nosferatu (1922): Silent German expressionist masterpiece.
  • Psycho (1960): The slasher precursor by Hitchcock.
  • The Exorcist (1973): Possession horror classic.
  • Get Out (2017): Modern social horror Oscar winner.
