Horror Icons Hall of Fame

Home | Types of Horror | Hall of Fame

Michael Myers

First introduced in John Carpenter's Halloween (1978), "The Shape" is an unstoppable silent stalker who terrorizes Haddonfield.

Jason Voorhees

The hockey-masked killer of Camp Crystal Lake who unleashes brutal vengeance in the Friday the 13th series.

Freddy Krueger

The burned dream demon with razor gloves who haunts and kills teenagers inside their nightmares in A Nightmare on Elm Street.

Ghostface

The iconic, movie-obsessed mask persona adopted by various killers in the Scream franchise.

Pennywise

An ancient shape-shifting entity that takes the form of a dancing clown to prey on the children of Derry, Maine in IT.

Leatherface

The chainsaw-wielding, human-skin mask wearing cannibal from the legendary Texas Chainsaw Massacre franchise.
