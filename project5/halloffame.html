Horror Hall of Fame

Six villains, six portraits. One short note per villain in the order they earned their place.

The Six Faces of the Hall of Fame


Michael Myers

Debut in Halloween (1978). Silent, unstoppable, and back every October.



Jason Voorhees

Debut in Friday the 13th (1980). Camp Crystal Lake's hockey-masked keeper.



Freddy Krueger

Debut in A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984). He kills you in your sleep.



Ghostface

Debut in Scream (1996). A different face behind the mask every time.



Pennywise

Debut in IT (1990). Returns every 27 years from the Derry sewers.



Leatherface

Debut in The Texas Chain Saw Massacre (1974). The original family butcher.
