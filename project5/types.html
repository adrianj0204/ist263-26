Types of Horror

Four subgenres, each with a short description and defining film example.


Slasher

A masked killer, a group of friends, and high stakes. The body count is high in this classic formula.

Defining Film: Halloween (1978)



Supernatural

Ghosts, haunted houses, or paranormal entities that cannot easily be reasoned with or banished.

Defining Film: The Exorcist (1973)



Psychological Horror

No physical monster required—just a mind coming apart where the viewer can no longer tell what is real.

Defining Film: The Shining (1980)



Found Footage

Shot on camcorders, phones, or dashcams, presented as recovered recordings from missing victims.

Defining Film: The Blair Witch Project (1999)
