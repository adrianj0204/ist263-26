Types of Horror

Explore the major subgenres that define horror cinema, from slashers and supernatural threats to psychological thrillers.

Slasher

A subgenre featuring a killer stalking and removing victims, often using edged weapons.

Defining Film: Halloween (1978)

Supernatural

Focuses on paranormal phenomena such as ghosts, demonic possessions, and hauntings.

Defining Film: The Exorcist (1973)

Psychological Horror

Relies on mental instability, paranoia, and psychological states to frighten the audience.

Defining Film: The Shining (1980)

Found Footage

Presented as discovered video recordings left behind by missing or deceased characters.

Defining Film: The Blair Witch Project (1999)
